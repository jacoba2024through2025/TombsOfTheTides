  
  
  # **Tomb of the Tides**

#### Tomb of the Tides is an action platformer in which you must navigate a perilous dungeon as water slowly rises below you. Jump, wall run and swing your way to escape before it's too late!

## **KEY FEATURES:**

The game features sprawling dungeon levels, parkour including wall jumping and wall running, a rising water level to evade, and other stage hazards such as spikes and turrets.

The player must also complete puzzles necessary to progress to the next level.

Tomb of The Tides has original soundtracks:
- Ancient Curse
- Main Menu Music
- Victory Song
- Thalassa
- Rolling Deep
- Sunken World


## **HOW TO USE (CLI)**
--Game also features custom keybinds--
WASD for movement.
Shift to sprint.
CTRL to crouch.
ALT to free look.
E to interact.
Space to jump/swim.
F to swing.
Tab to pause.
